A little magic mod I have created
# Features
## Seals & Spells
### Seals:
Normal Seal:
- can be used by anyone
- no consequences
- potency multiplier: 1
- cooldown divisor: 1
- craftable

Hellish Seal:
- can only be used by demons
- no consequences
- potency multiplier: 2
- cooldown divisor: 1
- obtained by pressing '\\' on an English keyboard or '#' on a german one, you need to be a demon

Golden Seal:
- can be used by anyone with more than one xp point
- takes xp relative to the cooldown of the used spell
- potency multiplier: 1
- cooldown divisor: 2
- craftable

Bloodsucking Seal:
- can be used by anyone
- applies some bleed buildup relative to the cooldown of the used spell
- potency multiplier: 2
- cooldown divisor: 1
- craftable

### Spell types:
Creative only Spells: \
obtained through the creative Menu

Highlighter Spell
  - toggles every Player's glowing

**Spells:**\
Standard Spells\
obtained through gazebos

Malignity
  - shoots a fireball with Explosion Power potency multiplier + 2 in the view direction of the caster
  - 300 / cooldown divisor Ticks Cooldown

Purloining:
  - dash in the movement direction
  - Usages = 5 * cooldown divisor
  - When usages left: 5 Ticks Cooldown
  - When usages depleted: 20 Ticks Cooldown on ground or 40 Ticks Cooldown in the  air

Guile:
  - makes the user invisible and throws a projectile, that spawns a cloud of smoke on impact
  - if the potency multiplier is more than 1, the cushioned status effect will be applied
  - smoke takes 800 / cooldown divisor Ticks to assimilate
  - 20 / cooldown divisor Ticks Cooldown

Appeasing:
  - Turns caster's food level into hearts
  - 300 / cooldown divisor Ticks Cooldown

Wickedness:
  - Returns the caster to their spawnpoint
  - 600 / cooldown divisor Ticks Cooldown

Envy:
  - Shoots an amethyst dart with high precision
  - 20 / cooldown divisor Ticks Cooldown

**Conjurings:**\
Brutal Spells mostly used by demons \
all obtained by pressing '\\' on an English keyboard or '#' on a german one, you need to be a demon

Treachery Judecca:
  - causes 120 * potency multiplier Ticks bleed buildup
  - kills non-living entities immediately
  - 3 / cooldown divisor Ticks Cooldown

Emulations:
  - fixates and impales nearest entity with 6 Projectiles, every 25 Ticks
  - causes 1200 * potencyMultiplier - 80 Ticks bleed buildup
  - 600 / cooldown divisor Ticks Cooldown (or 20 / cooldown divisor on fail)

Heresies:
  - Teleports either all entities around the caster, or the caster themselves in and out of the pocket dimension
  - no cooldown except an animation when teleporting to the pocket dimension (do not step out of the box!) 

**Sorceries:**\
Powerful Spells, where the seal itself becomes a tool as well\
obtained through the gazebos after obtaining all spells

Combustion:
  - when hitting an entity with the seal, they explode
    - 100 / cooldown divisor Ticks Cooldown
  - using the seal creates a laser made of flames
    - no cooldown

Current:
  - using the seal continuously will spawn a lighting bolt spanning between the start and end point,
    dealing high damage when walked through
  - 100 / cooldown divisor Ticks Cooldown

Kinesis:
  - throws every entity in the range (even through walls!) in the rotation movement of the caster
  - deals low damage continuously while using
  - no cooldown
